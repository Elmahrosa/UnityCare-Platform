iv.

Dockerized microservices, Railway CI/CD, health checks, and monitoring stubs for Prometheus and Grafana.

Operating Principles

Three commitments that shape every deployment.

i.

Sovereign by design

Patient data, code, and credentials stay inside the institution's perimeter. Self-hosted by default — we pull only what's strictly required to run.

ii.

Built for institutions

Our buyers are hospital groups and health ministries, not individual clinics. Every capability is staged for pilot, then license, then full ownership transfer.

iii.

Audit over assurance

We don't ask institutions to trust a dashboard. The hash-chained ledger lets any party verify what happened, independent of the vendor.
